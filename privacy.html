Privacy, by design.

Warranty AI is provided by Castaldo Studio. Your warranty records, receipt images, recognized text, and coverage questions are processed and stored on your iPhone.

Information handled by the app

You may add product names, purchase prices, stores, purchase and expiration dates, warranty terms, receipt images, and questions. We do not operate a server that receives this information. Receipt recognition uses Apple Vision on-device. Supported generative coverage answers use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework; there is no developer-operated cloud AI or paid AI API fallback.

Local storage and deletion

Records and receipts are stored in the app’s protected Documents folder. App preferences are stored locally. Delete an item to remove its record and saved receipt from the app. Removing the app can delete its local data. iOS backups may include app data according to your device and Apple Account settings; exported copies and backups are controlled separately by you and Apple.

Camera, photos, notifications, and calendar

The camera is used only when you choose to scan. The photo picker shares only images you select. Notification permission is optional and supports local expiration reminders. Calendar events are created through Apple’s editor after your confirmation. These features do not give the developer remote access to your camera, photo library, or calendar.

Purchases

Payments, subscriptions, restoration, and refunds are handled by Apple. The app reads Apple’s verified transaction information on-device to determine access. We do not receive your payment-card information. Apple’s privacy policy applies to Apple’s services.

Analytics and tracking

The app has no advertising SDK, developer analytics service, cross-app tracking, or sale of personal data. No app account is required.

Information you choose to share

When you export a receipt, you choose the destination through the iPhone share sheet. That destination’s privacy practices apply. If you contact support by email, we receive the message, your email address, and any attachments you send. We use that information to respond and resolve the request and retain it only as reasonably necessary for support and applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence where applicable.

This support website

This website is hosted separately from the app. Its hosting provider may process standard request information needed to deliver and protect the website. The pages do not include advertising or analytics scripts.

Children and changes

Warranty AI is a general-purpose organization tool and is not specifically directed at children. We may update this policy when the app’s practices change, and will publish an updated effective date here.
