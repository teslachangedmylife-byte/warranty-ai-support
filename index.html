Support

Scan and save

Choose Scan Receipt or Warranty. Use the camera, import a photo, or enter details manually. Recognition happens on your iPhone. Review the suggested product, store, price and date, and confirm expiration against your original warranty before saving. Multi-page camera scans are stored together as a PDF.

Find or export a receipt

Open My Items, select a product and choose View saved receipt. Export Receipt opens the iPhone share sheet. Records are stored on-device. Back up important receipts before removing the app; restoring a purchase does not restore deleted receipt files.

Coverage AI

Add the original warranty terms in Edit Item. Supported iPhones with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model ready use Apple's on-device model. Other devices use a clearly labeled offline guide that matches text in saved terms. There is no paid AI API or cloud AI fallback. Guidance can be incomplete or incorrect. The retailer or manufacturer confirms actual coverage.

Reminders and calendar

Enable warranty notifications to request a local reminder at 9 am, 30 days before expiration. Notification delivery depends on your iPhone settings and Focus modes. Calendar events are saved only when you confirm Apple's editor.

Preview, plans and restoration

Explore all features for three days from Get Started. The preview does not automatically start a subscription or charge you. Afterward, PRO is required to use the app. Choose monthly, yearly or lifetime access. Apple displays the actual price and asks you to confirm payment. Use Restore Purchases with the original Apple Account. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026. Warranty AI is provided by Castaldo Studio. Privacy contact: teslachangedmylife@gmail.com.

Your information stays on-device

The app handles the product names, stores, prices, dates, warranty terms, receipt images, recognized text and questions you supply. We do not operate a server that receives these records. Apple Vision performs receipt recognition on-device. Supported generative answers use Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework; there is no developer-operated cloud AI service or API fallback.

Storage, deletion and backups

Items and receipts are stored in the app's protected Documents folder. Preferences and the preview start time are stored locally. Deleting an item removes its record and saved receipt. Removing the app can delete its data. iOS backups may include app data depending on your settings. Exported copies and backups are managed separately by you and Apple.

Permissions and purchases

The camera is used when you choose to scan. The photo picker shares only selected images. Notifications are optional. Calendar events are created through Apple's editor after confirmation. These features give us no remote access to your camera, photo library or calendar. Apple handles payments, refunds and subscriptions. The app reads verified purchase information on-device to determine access. We do not receive payment-card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple's services.

Tracking and sharing

The app has no advertising SDK, developer analytics service, cross-app tracking or sale of personal data. No app account is required. When you export a receipt, you select its destination and that destination's privacy practices apply.

Support correspondence and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments. We use them to respond and resolve the request, retaining them only as reasonably necessary for support and applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion where applicable. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process standard request information needed to deliver and protect the website. These pages include no analytics or advertising scripts.

Three-day preview

The app preview starts at Get Started and lasts three days. It does not automatically enroll you in a subscription or charge you. PRO is required to use app features after it ends. An internet connection may be required for Apple purchase verification or restoration.

Monthly, yearly and lifetime access

PRO is available through a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or a lifetime non-consumable purchase. Prices and periods are displayed in the app and confirmed by Apple in your storefront currency. Lifetime unlocks PRO in Warranty AI without a recurring subscription fee. It is not insurance or a guarantee of third-party warranty coverage.

Billing, cancellation and refunds

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm a purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel them in your Apple Account subscription settings. Apple handles billing and refund requests under its applicable terms. Restore Purchases restores eligible access using the original Apple Account; it does not restore receipt data.

Coverage guidance and your records

The app organizes information supplied by you. Recognition and guidance can be wrong or incomplete; review the original warranty document. Warranty AI does not provide insurance, determine legal rights, guarantee repairs or eligibility, book repairs, or submit claims. The retailer or manufacturer makes coverage decisions. Keep backups of important receipts and warranties. You control the records you enter and the files you choose to export.
