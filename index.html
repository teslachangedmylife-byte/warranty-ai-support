Scan and save a warranty

Choose Scan Receipt or Warranty, then use the camera, import a photo, or enter details manually. The app recognizes text on your iPhone. Review suggested details and confirm the expiration against your original warranty before saving. Multi-page camera scans are saved together as a PDF.

Where are my receipts?

Open My Items, select a product, then choose View saved receipt. Export Receipt opens the iPhone share sheet. Items and receipts are stored on your device; they are not uploaded to our servers. Back up important receipts before removing the app.

How does Coverage AI work?

Add the original warranty terms in Edit Item. On supported iPhones with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model ready, answers use Apple’s on-device model. Other devices receive a clearly labeled offline guide that matches text in the terms. No paid AI API is used. Guidance cannot guarantee coverage; the retailer or manufacturer confirms eligibility.

Reminders and calendar events

Enable warranty notifications in Settings to request a reminder 30 days before expiration, at 9 am. Permission is optional. Calendar events are saved only when you confirm Apple’s calendar editor. Notification delivery can depend on your iPhone settings and Focus modes.

Explore free for three days

Choose Start My 3 Free Days on the welcome screen, then Skip for Now on the plans screen to explore all features without buying. This app preview lasts 72 hours from that first start and does not charge automatically. After it expires, the plans screen stays in place until you purchase or restore PRO. Opening the welcome screen again does not restart the preview.

Subscription trials and purchases

Eligible new subscribers can start an Apple-managed three-day free trial on Monthly or Yearly PRO after confirming with Apple. After three days, the selected subscription is charged automatically unless canceled. U.S. prices are $6.99 per month or $49.99 per year; your local price and eligibility are confirmed by Apple. Lifetime costs $149.99 in the U.S., charged once when confirmed, with no subscription trial or renewal. PRO is required after the app preview expires.

Restore or manage a purchase

Use Restore Purchases on the PRO screen with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Manage monthly or yearly subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Reinstalling can remove local receipt data; restoring a purchase does not restore receipts.
