Three-day app preview

All features are available for a 72-hour preview starting when you first choose Start My 3 Free Days. You may choose Skip for Now instead of buying during this preview. Subscription free trial and access

Eligible new subscribers may receive an Apple-managed three-day free trial with a Monthly or Yearly PRO subscription. The trial begins only after you confirm the subscription with Apple. When it ends, the selected subscription price is charged automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Apple determines introductory-offer eligibility; only one introductory offer is available per subscription group. PRO access is required after the app preview expires. The local app preview is separate from an Apple subscription introductory offer. An internet connection may be needed for Apple purchase verification and restoration.

Monthly, yearly, and lifetime

PRO can be purchased as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or as a lifetime non-consumable purchase. Prices and billing periods are displayed in the app and confirmed by Apple in your storefront currency. Lifetime unlocks PRO in Warranty AI without a recurring subscription fee; it is not an insurance policy or a guarantee of third-party warranty coverage.

Subscription billing

For an eligible free trial, subscription payment begins after three days unless canceled. Without a trial, payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm a purchase. U.S. prices are $6.99 monthly, $49.99 yearly, and $149.99 for Lifetime. Apple displays the actual storefront price and purchase terms. Lifetime is charged once when confirmed and does not include a subscription free trial. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account subscription settings. Apple handles billing, cancellation, and refund requests under its applicable terms. Restore Purchases can restore eligible access using the original Apple Account.

Warranty guidance

The app organizes information supplied by you. Receipt recognition and AI or offline guidance can be incomplete or incorrect. Review extracted fields and use the original warranty document as your source. The app does not provide insurance, guarantee a repair, determine legal rights, or submit or book service requests. Contact the retailer or manufacturer to confirm coverage and eligibility.
